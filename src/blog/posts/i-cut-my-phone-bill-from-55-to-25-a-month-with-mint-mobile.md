**Starting small: the snowball approach to cutting costs**

Have you heard of the snowball method for paying off debt? You pay off the smallest debts first, and the quick wins motivate you to keep going. I think something similar works for cutting costs. Starting small feels less daunting to me, so I'm starting with my phone bill.

**What I was paying**

I was paying $55 a month for T-Mobile's pay-as-you-go plan with unlimited calls and texts. While that's not outrageous, I'm committed to taking a closer look at every budget item, no matter how I initially feel about it.

**Switching to Mint Mobile**

After some quick googling, I found a cheaper option. I was a little worried about spotty service, but this is all an experiment, after all, so I wanted to give it a try.

I switched to Mint Mobile and paid $297.11 upfront for a year of unlimited service. That comes out to $24.76 a month.

**Why an unlocked phone made switching easy**

I bought my current phone unlocked from the Apple Store years ago so I wouldn't be tied to a contract and could use local SIM cards when traveling. Because of that, switching to Mint was as simple as activating a new SIM, with no carrier to get permission from first. 

**Has the service been worse?**

Honestly, I haven't noticed a difference at all. That makes sense: Mint runs on T-Mobile's network, so I'm on the same towers I was using before, for less than half the price.

**What I'm saving**

Switching saves me $30.24 a month, or $362.88 a year. That one change is worth more than half of a nursing shift. Glad to have those hours back.
